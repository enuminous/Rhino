# Limitations and Falsification

RHINO is an experimental robustness framework.

## Limitations

- Novelty is difficult to quantify.
- Stress generators may not resemble real incidents.
- State-distance metrics may be arbitrary.
- Invariant sets can be incomplete.
- Very severe shocks may make comparisons meaningless.
- Recovery potential is only an estimate until recovery is attempted.

## Falsification criteria

RHINO's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Conventional stress testing captures the same outcomes.
2. Invariant survival adds no predictive value.
3. Shock amplification does not correlate with real fragility.
4. Novel held-out shocks do not change conclusions.
5. Recovery-potential estimates fail to predict actual recovery.
6. Results are unstable across reasonable perturbation encodings.

## Claim boundary

RHINO evaluates robustness under specified perturbations. It does not prove universal resilience.
